A small village was located near a quiet river surrounded by green fields and tall trees. Every morning, farmers walked toward their fields while birds moved between the branches. The village had a simple market where people purchased vegetables, fruits, grains, and household items. One summer, the river began to become smaller because there had been very little rainfall. The villagers realized that they needed to manage their water carefully. They started collecting rainwater, repairing old wells, and avoiding unnecessary water usage. After several weeks, dark clouds appeared in the sky and heavy rain finally arrived. The river slowly returned to its normal level. The experience taught the villagers that natural resources should never be taken for granted and that cooperation can help communities solve difficult problems.
